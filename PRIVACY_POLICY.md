# Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 3, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what information the Game Finder Discord bot ("the Bot") processes and how it is handled.

## 1. Information We Process
To provide game recommendations, the Bot processes the following data:
- **Steam ID**: The numeric Steam ID you provide or the one retrieved from a Steam URL you provide.
- **Steam Profile Data**: Publicly available lists of your recently played games and owned games (via the Steam API).
- **Discord Metadata**: Basic interaction data required by Discord (User ID, Server ID) to execute commands.

## 2. How We Use Information
The information is used solely to:
- Fetch your gaming history from Steam.
- Generate recommendations using Google's Gemini AI.
- Send the recommendations back to your Discord channel.

## 3. Data Storage
**We do not store your data.**
- Steam IDs and game lists are processed in memory during the execution of commands.
- Once the recommendation is delivered and the command execution finishes, this data is cleared from the Bot's memory.
- We do not maintain a database of users or their gaming habits.

## 4. Third-Party Sharing
To generate recommendations, the list of games you have played/own is sent to **Google's Gemini AI API**. This data is shared only for the purpose of generating a recommendation. Please refer to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for how they handle API data.

## 5. Data Security
We use industry-standard practices to keep the Bot's connection to third-party services secure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

## 6. Your Rights
Since we do not store your data, there is no data to delete or modify. To stop the Bot from processing your data, simply stop using the Bot's commands or remove the Bot from your server.

## 7. Contact
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the bot developer.
